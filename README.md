One job title, 60 characters at most, the weirdest one we could think of:

> Senior Penguin Relocation Coordinator, Antarctica

It reads like a real conservation job posting — a senior coordinator role —
with one hilariously specific twist: the thing being relocated is penguins,
in Antarctica. Real-sounding role, one precise absurd detail, nothing else.

`main.py` writes exactly that title (49 characters, no trailing newline)
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network, stdlib only.
